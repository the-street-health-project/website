"Providing basic medical care with dignity." — founded and run by high school students in Iowa.

Basic care should not depend on where you sleep at night.

An estimated 17,000+ Iowans lack stable housing or reliable access to basic hygiene supplies. A toothbrush, clean gauze, or a five-minute screening can be the difference between a small problem and an emergency room visit, so that's where we start.

What we do

Two programs. One goal.

We meet immediate physical needs and long-term health needs at the same time, through supplies people can use today and tools that catch problems early.

Medical kits

Medical supply kits

We assemble hygiene and first-aid kits by hand and distribute them to unhoused individuals across the Cedar Rapids area. Each kit covers wound care, hygiene, and basic comfort items for someone living without steady access to either.

Refurbished tech

Refurbished laptops with health screening

We collect donated computers, wipe them securely, and refurbish them for distribution alongside our kits. Each laptop runs on-device screening software that checks for signs of jaundice and anemia. It is a screening tool, not a diagnosis, and it never sends data anywhere. All processing happens on the device itself.

How it works

From donation to delivery

01

Collect

We gather supplies, cash donations, and donated laptops from local businesses, schools, and community partners.

02

Prepare

Volunteers assemble kits by hand and refurbish each laptop, wiping it clean and loading our screening software.

03

Distribute

We deliver kits and laptops directly to unhoused individuals across Cedar Rapids and Marion.

04

Screen

Anyone with a laptop can run a free, private jaundice or anemia screening and know when to see a doctor.

What's next

We are growing past Cedar Rapids.

Our next phase takes the same model, supply kits paired with refurbished tech, and applies it to communities beyond Iowa. Here is where we stand.

Statewide expansion

We are reaching out to school districts across Iowa for surplus laptop donations, and to local businesses for funding and supply donations.

  • 26 Iowa school districts contacted
  • 150+ businesses and organizations reached
  • State surplus property program identified as a donation channel

BiliScan development

We continue to build out our on-device screening software, adding new checks while keeping every scan private and offline.

  • Jaundice and anemia screening live
  • Cyanosis and capillary refill checks in development
  • Built for accuracy across skin tones
Completed · first international project

From Iowa to Kanchipuram, India

The Street Health Project completed its first international donation: new hygiene and basic first-aid supplies, delivered to Lions Club of Kanchipuram Host in Tamil Nadu, India. The club will put the supplies to use through its own hospital and community service programs.

TSHP founders Arya Ravikumar and Dakshesh "Dakshi" Kondiboyina organized and coordinated the project, reaching out to the club directly through their website. Ravikumar Devarajan and his brother Rajasekar Devarajan volunteered to help hand-deliver the donation in Kanchipuram.

  • Partner Lions Club of Kanchipuram Host, District 3241 H
  • Organized by Arya Ravikumar & Dakshesh Kondiboyina, TSHP founders
  • Delivered by Ravikumar Devarajan & Rajasekar Devarajan, volunteers
  • Received by Ln. K. Saravanan, Service Chairperson
  • Next step Building toward more partnerships like this one
