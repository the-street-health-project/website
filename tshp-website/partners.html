Partners & sponsors

Every kit starts with someone’s donation.

The Street Health Project runs on donated supplies and community partnership. Here’s who has supported our work, and exactly what they gave.

See sponsorship levels Donations in photos
Supply donor

Blair Ridge Dental

Blair Ridge Dental donated toothbrushes and toothpaste (Delta Dental–branded products, donated through Blair Ridge Dental’s office). They cover the dental-care part of our kits.

  • Toothbrushes
  • Toothpaste
Supply donor

His Hands Free Clinic

His Hands Free Clinic donated wound-care supplies that went straight into our kits.

  • Gauze pads
  • Abdominal pads
  • Medical tape
  • Lip balm
International partner

Lions Club of Kanchipuram Host

Lions Club of Kanchipuram Host (District 3241 H) received our first international donation: 264 new hygiene and basic first-aid supplies. The club will use them through its hospital and community service activities.
