Questions

Good things to know.

Can I give less than $100?

Absolutely. Every $8 helps make one kit, and you can give any amount on GoFundMe. Levels just help us thank bigger sponsors properly.

Does a level have to be one payment?

No. If you’d like to give over time or combine gifts, tell us and we’ll work it out together.

Can my business donate supplies instead of money?

Yes. Items from our kit list are always welcome, whether it’s a one-time box or a regular drop-off. Send us a note with what you have.

How will I know where my gift went?

We post our kit drives and deliveries on Instagram, and every donation photo is credited to the organization that gave it. From Guardian up, we’ll also send you photo updates directly.

Can I get a receipt?

GoFundMe emails a receipt for every online gift. For sponsorships, contact us and we’ll send written confirmation of your gift.
