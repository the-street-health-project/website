Completed · first international project

From Iowa to Kanchipuram, India.

The Street Health Project completed its first international donation: new hygiene and basic first-aid supplies, donated to Lions Club of Kanchipuram Host in Tamil Nadu, India. The club will use them through its own hospital and community service activities.

Our founders, Arya Ravikumar and Dakshesh “Dakshi” Kondiboyina, organized the project, reaching out to the club directly through its website. Ravikumar Devarajan and his brother, Rajasekar Devarajan, volunteered to hand-deliver the donation in Kanchipuram.

The donation

One delivery. Seven kinds of essential supplies.

These are the exact quantities, matching the list in the club’s own thank-you. We donated them to the Lions Club, which puts them to use through its own hospital and community service programs.

  • 72Toothbrushes
  • 73Crest toothpaste
  • 45Medicated chapsticks
  • 50Sterile gauze pads
  • 16Abdominal pads
  • 4Gauze bandages
  • 4Cloth surgical tapes
  • 264Supplies in total
Step 1 · Packing in Iowa

Arya and Dakshi gathered the supplies and packed every box by hand before the donation left for India.

Step 2 · Delivered in Kanchipuram

Volunteers handed the supplies over in person at Lions Club of Kanchipuram Host, where Ln. K. Saravanan, Service Chairperson, received them for the club.

Step 3 · A thank-you from Kanchipuram

The club shared a public thank-you, in Tamil and English, and printed the news in its bulletin.
