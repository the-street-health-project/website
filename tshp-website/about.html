About us

Started by two students. Powered by a community.

The Street Health Project is a student-led nonprofit in Cedar Rapids and Marion, Iowa. We collect donated medical and hygiene supplies, pack them into kits, and get them to the people who need them most.

Our mission

Giving back to the community we live in.

It’s estimated that more than 17,000 people in Iowa lack basic necessities and housing. We can’t fix that on our own, but we can make sure the basics are within reach: a toothbrush, clean gauze, a pair of socks.

We’re run by high school students, and we do the work ourselves. We ask local businesses and clinics for donations, sort the supplies, pack every kit by hand, and bring the kits to unhoused neighbors and local shelters.

We’ve also completed our first international donation, to Lions Club of Kanchipuram Host in India. It’s a first step, and we’re proud of it.

Our team

The people behind every kit.

Our two co-founders lead the work day to day, alongside 8+ student volunteers who help pack kits and run donation drives. Together, the founders also organized our first international donation, to Kanchipuram, India.

What we believe

How we try to do this right.

Dignity first

Everyone deserves the basics. We pack every kit with care and hand it over with respect, because that’s the whole point.

Credit where it’s due
